The Core Challenge & Solution

This section provides a high-level overview of the architectural approach required to serve native Dagbani speakers, emphasizing the necessity of an audio-first interaction layer.

⚠ The Problem

Dagbani has a rich cultural heritage, but it is primarily an oral language. While a written standard exists, daily communication, folklore, and rural interaction rely heavily on speech. A standard text-in/text-out LLM would suffer from low adoption rates among the native population.

  • Scarcity of digitized written text.
  • Low literacy rates in the standardized orthography.
  • High context dependency in spoken nuances.

💡 The Architecture

The solution is a composite AI pipeline. We must build a traditional text-based LLM, but encapsulate it entirely within a robust Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) shell.

Language Model Architecture

Training an LLM from scratch requires billions of tokens, which Dagbani currently lacks. The optimal strategy relies on Transfer Learning and Continuous Pre-training on existing multilingual models.

🏢

1. Select Base Model

Llama-3 (8B), Aya-23, or Mistral. Models with good multilingual foundations.

📚

2. Vocabulary Expansion

Add specific Dagbani characters/tokens to the tokenizer to prevent text fragmentation.

🔧

3. LoRA / QLoRA

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning on high-quality Dagbani instruction pairs.

Why this works: The model already "understands" logic, reasoning, and grammar from English/French. We are teaching it to map those abstract concepts to the Dagbani linguistic structure, requiring drastically less data (approx. 50k - 100k high-quality conversational pairs).
❌

Not Recommended for Dagbani

Training a foundational model from scratch typically requires trillions of tokens. The entire digitized text available for Dagbani is estimated to be less than 50 million tokens. A model trained from scratch on this data would suffer from severe overfitting and fail to grasp basic logic.

Text-to-Speech (TTS) Integration

The TTS layer is the most critical component for user adoption. It must sound natural and respect local dialects (e.g., Tomosili vs. Nayahali).

TTS Development Steps

  • 1
    Phonetic Dictionary

    Map Dagbani text to IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet). Crucial for tonal accuracy.

  • 2
    Studio Recording

    20+ hours of clean, phonetically balanced recordings by a native voice actor.

  • 3
    Model Selection

    Train using VITS (end-to-end) or FastSpeech2 + HiFi-GAN for fast, natural inference.
